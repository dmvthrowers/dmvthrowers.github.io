GOVERNING DOCUMENT

Club Charter

DMV Throwers Yo-Yo & Skill Toy Club · Charter v5 · DC · MD · VA

Article I — Name & Identity
Section 1.1 — Official Name

The official name of this organization is DMV Throwers Yo-Yo & Skill Toy Club, hereinafter referred to as "The YoYo Club" or "DMV Throwers."

Section 1.2 — Geographic Scope

The Club serves the greater Washington, DC metropolitan area, encompassing the District of Columbia, Maryland, and Virginia (DMV region).

Section 1.3 — Online Identity

The Club operates under the handle @DMV_THROWERS across social media platforms and may be contacted at Dmvthrowers@gmail.com. Additional information is available at linktr.ee/dmvthrowers.

Article II — Mission & Purpose
Section 2.1 — Mission Statement

DMV Throwers exists to build and grow a welcoming community of yo-yo enthusiasts and skill toy players across the DMV region — connecting beginners, hobbyists, and advanced players of all ages.

  • Organize regular public meetups where members and newcomers can practice, learn, and connect
  • Provide free, accessible events open to all ages and skill levels
  • Promote yo-yo, kendama, diabolo, juggling, and related skill toys as legitimate hobbies and competitive pursuits
  • Support skill development through informal coaching and trick-sharing at meetups
  • Foster a family-friendly, inclusive environment where all are welcome regardless of experience
  • Represent the DMV yo-yo community at regional and national events and competitions
Article III — Membership
Section 3.1 — Open Membership

Membership is open to any person with an interest in yo-yo or skill toys, regardless of age, experience level, or background. There are no formal application requirements or fees.

Section 3.2 — Membership Tiers
TierDescription
Community Member (Default)Anyone who attends a DMV Throwers event. No registration required. All are welcome.
Active MemberA person who regularly participates in Club activities, contributes to planning or operations, or assists at events. Active members may weigh in on Club decisions.
Leadership MemberAn officer who holds a formal role within the Club (see Article V).
Section 3.3 — No Dues Policy

Attendance at all DMV Throwers events is and shall remain free of charge. The Club does not charge membership dues. Voluntary donations are welcomed and appreciated, and will be used to support Club operations, supplies, and events. Donations are never required and confer no special status.

Section 3.4 — Code of Conduct

All members and attendees are expected to uphold the following:

  • Treat all people with respect — members, newcomers, children, and families alike
  • Create a welcoming environment regardless of skill level, age, or background
  • Avoid disruptive, harassing, or unsafe behavior at any Club event or venue
  • Represent DMV Throwers positively in person and online
  • Follow the rules and policies of any venue hosting a Club event
  • Help newcomers feel included — share knowledge freely and encourage beginners

Violations of the Code of Conduct may result in removal from an event or suspension from Club activities. The President may act on this unilaterally. The Vice President may act independently as well, subject to presidential veto. All other officers may act only with the concurrence of at least one additional officer, the President, or the Vice President.

Article IV — Meetings & Events
Section 4.1 — Regular Monthly Meetup

The Club shall hold a regular monthly meetup on the 3rd Sunday of each month from 1:00 PM to 4:00 PM. The primary indoor venue is the Arlington Central Library (Barbara M. Donnellan Auditorium / Bluemont Room), 1015 N Quincy St, Arlington, VA 22201, subject to availability.

Section 4.2 — Outdoor & Alternative Events

The Club may organize outdoor meetups (e.g., Quincy Park, Arlington, VA) during favorable weather months, or use alternative venues as needed. Venue changes will be communicated through official channels at least one week in advance when possible.

Section 4.3 — Special Events

The Club may organize additional special meetups, themed sessions, collaborative events, or participation in external competitions and festivals. Special events may be authorized by the President or Vice President acting independently. Other officers may propose and organize special events with the approval of at least one additional officer, the President, or the Vice President.

Section 4.5 — Event Standards

All standard Club meetups shall be free to attend with no registration required (RSVP may be requested for planning), open to all ages and skill levels, family-friendly, and operated with loaner yo-yos available when feasible. Events involving competition or significant production costs may charge fees limited to covering event-specific expenses, with authorization by the President or Vice President.

Section 4.6 — Cancellations

Events may be cancelled or postponed due to venue unavailability, severe weather, or unforeseen circumstances. Cancellations will be announced on official social media channels as early as possible.

Article V — Leadership & Officers
Section 5.1 — Officer Roles
RolePrimary Responsibilities
Founder / PresidentHolds full autonomous authority over all Club decisions, operations, and direction. May act unilaterally on any matter at any time. Final authority is never subject to override except by the Founder themselves.
Coordinator / Vice PresidentOperates the Club with full authority in the President's absence or as delegated. May act independently on Club matters as they see fit. The President retains the right to veto any Vice President decision if deemed necessary.
Co-Coordinator / ModeratorAssists with event coordination and community moderation. Supports active events and helps manage Club communications and member relations.
Alt. Location Co-CoordinatorCoordinates logistics for secondary or alternate event locations. Serves as on-site lead when events are held outside the primary venue.
Moderator / Activity CoordinatorPlans and facilitates in-event activities such as trick challenges, workshops, and themed sessions. Assists with community engagement.
Section 5.3 — Authority & Decision Making

The Club operates under a three-tier authority structure:

  • President / Founder: Full autonomous authority. May act unilaterally on any Club matter without approval from any other officer.
  • Vice President / Coordinator: Full operational authority to run the Club as they see fit. The President may veto any Vice President decision if deemed necessary. No other officer may override the Vice President.
  • All Other Officers: May act on Club matters only with the concurrence of at least one additional officer, the President, or the Vice President.
Article VI — Communications & Social Media
Section 6.1 — Official Channels
  • Social Media: @DMV_THROWERS
  • Email: Dmvthrowers@gmail.com
  • Linktree: linktr.ee/dmvthrowers
Section 6.3 — Photography & Media

Photos and videos taken at Club events may be shared on official channels to promote the community. Members who do not wish to be photographed should inform an officer. Photos of minors shall not be shared without reasonable consent from a guardian.

Article VII — Amendments

This charter may be amended by the President acting alone, or by the Vice President acting independently (subject to presidential veto). Amendments proposed by other officers require the concurrence of at least one additional officer, the President, or the Vice President. All amendments should be documented in writing and communicated to the full officer team. The Club should review this charter annually, typically at the start of a new calendar year.

Article VIII — Dissolution

In the unlikely event of Club dissolution, all remaining Club assets — including materials, equipment, and any donated funds — shall be donated to another local yo-yo, skill toy, or hobby club in the DMV region; donated to a related nonprofit or community organization; or distributed to active members as determined at the time of dissolution. No assets shall be distributed for personal financial gain.

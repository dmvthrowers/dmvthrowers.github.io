Today’s Standard Setup
  • Bearing: C size (¼″ × ½″ × ⅜″), the main size for competition yo-yos. Centering (concave) designs like the KonKave keep the string in the middle of the bearing.[21][2][46]
  • Response: 19mm “Slim” silicone pads, the most common size, about 1–1.1mm thick, or flowable silicone in the groove.[8][44][14]
  • String: 100% polyester, now preferred over cotton. Neon yellow and white are the most popular colors.[14][53][34]
  • Lube: optional. A drop of thin lube keeps a bearing quiet and protected; it does not clean it.[14][50]

Quick Troubleshooting

From our Maintenance Guide and the YoYoExpert forums[1]
ProblemLikely causeFix
Slips or won’t returnResponse pad worn or fell outClean the groove and replace the pad
Slips or won’t returnBearing needs lubeAdd thick yo-yo lube
Won’t sleepString installed wrong, or a knot at the axleRe-install or replace the string
Won’t sleepString tension too tight, dirty bearing, string too thick, or pads sticking outFix tension, clean the bearing, or swap to thinner string or pads[204][205]
Suddenly responsiveDirty bearingDo the chase test, then clean the bearing[206]
Won’t screw togetherStripped threads from over-tightening or cross-threadingTry a longer axle or a new axle; glue the axle into a stripped half; tighten only until snug[196][197]
Bearing won’t come offTight or galled bearing seatNeedle-nose pliers and a wiggle, or the string trick[201]
Vibes after a dropAxle or bearing seated off-centerFlip the axle and bearing, retighten evenly[213]

Work through it in order: pads, then bearing lube, then string. Most problems fall into one of those three.[1] Download the full DMV Throwers Maintenance Guide.

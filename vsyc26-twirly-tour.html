Goodles Twirly Mac on Tour

Goodles is taking Twirly Mac on the road, and VSYC-26 is the Virginia stop. Same contest, same free admission — with the Goodles sampling cart, stickers, temporary tattoos, and Twirl for a Prize on the floor all day.

The tour celebrates the launch of Twirly Mac and kicked off with a roller party in Santa Cruz, Goodles’ hometown. From there it runs through festivals of twirl across the country. Below is where it goes; we can only speak for ours.

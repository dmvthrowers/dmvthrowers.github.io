ABOUT THE EVENT

Virginia's Yo-Yo Contest
Comes to Northern VA

The Virginia State Yo-Yo Contest has been a Virginia tradition since 2007. This year we're bringing it to Northern Virginia for the first time — Center Court at Dulles Town Center, Saturday crowds, and a community that genuinely loves what they do.

Emceed by Brian Melford, keeping the day on schedule from kickoff to closing ceremony.

Stella Duellum, hosted by Anneurismz for Prismatic Stars, ran at 12:00 PM with its own sign-up — rules and results here.

Entirely volunteer-run. Every dollar goes straight to the event. Buttons, shirts, and other merch are for sale on the day — see what’s available — proceeds go toward running future Mid-Atlantic yo-yo contests. Organized by DMV Throwers Yo-Yo & Skill Toy Club.

The day-of livestream ran on the YoYo Contest Central YouTube channel, and the full replay is up there now, along with contest videos.

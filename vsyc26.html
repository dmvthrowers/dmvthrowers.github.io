ABOUT THE EVENT

Virginia's Yo-Yo Contest
Comes to Northern VA

The Virginia State Yo-Yo Contest has run every year since 2007. This year we're bringing it to Northern Virginia for the first time — Center Court at Dulles Town Center, Saturday crowds, and a community that genuinely loves what they do.

Entirely volunteer-run. Every dollar goes straight to the event. Organized by DMV Throwers Yo-Yo & Skill Toy Club.

CONTEST RULES

Contest Ruleset

FREESTYLE FORMAT
  • Freestyles follow IYYF/AYYA guidelines unless otherwise noted
  • 1A, X Division: 3-minute freestyle with music
  • Beginner/Junior: Shorter format — TBD based on registration
  • Fixed Axle: Format TBD — announcement coming soon
  • No profanity or offensive content in music
EQUIPMENT
  • Competitors must use division-legal yo-yos for their registered division
  • Fixed Axle: No bearing yo-yos — traditional axle only
  • String and accessories are competitor's responsibility
  • Inspections may occur before competition at judge's discretion
MUSIC & PERFORMANCE
  • Music files due by September 12 — no exceptions after deadline
  • File format: MP3, named DIVISION_Last_First.mp3
  • No live music — pre-recorded tracks only
  • Competitors must be ready when called — missing your slot forfeits your spot
JUDGING & CONDUCT
  • Judging follows standard competitive yo-yo scoring criteria
  • Judge decisions are final
  • Respectful conduct required — unsportsmanlike behavior may result in disqualification
  • Venue rules apply to all competitors, spectators, and staff
  • Organizers reserve the right to adjust format based on registration numbers

FAQ

Common Questions

Is the event free to attend?+
Yes — free to spectate. Competitor entry is $15–25 per division depending on division. No charge for spectators, families, or curious mall visitors.
How do I register to compete?+
Registration opens soon. Email vastateyoyocontest@gmail.com to get notified first. Registration is only complete after payment clears.
Can I compete in multiple divisions?+
Yes. Registration is per division. A discount applies for each additional division — details in the registration form.
What is the Maker Corner?+
A community showcase area for handmade skill toys, custom designs, and indie crafted work. Not a sponsor table — this is community space for makers. Contact us if you're interested in a spot.
Will there be a livestream?+
Planned — pending funding. Diamond and Platinum sponsors receive YouTube livestream credit. Once funded, it's happening. Help us get there.
Where do I park?+
Free parking at all mall entrances. Dulles Town Center has ample surface parking surrounding the entire mall. No fees, no validation needed.
How do I become a sponsor?+
Fill out the sponsor interest form above or email vastateyoyocontest@gmail.com. Diamond and Platinum spots are limited — first come, first served. You'll receive a PayPal invoice to lock your spot.
Who organizes VSYC-26?+
DMV Throwers Yo-Yo & Skill Toy Club — the DC/Maryland/Virginia yo-yo community club. Entirely volunteer-run. Every dollar goes straight to the event.

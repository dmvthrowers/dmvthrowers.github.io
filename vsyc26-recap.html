YOUR FEEDBACK

What You Told Us

Spectators rated the day 4.2 out of 5, and every survey respondent said they'd come back, many with friends in tow. Your favorite parts were the competitor routines, the vendor tables, the crowd energy and the Goodles booth. You also told us what didn't work, and here's what we're changing.

What we heardWhat we're changing
The schedule ran long and it was hard to know what was nextA live schedule board by the stage, and a set lunch break
The yo-yo battles ran too long in the middle of the dayBattles move to the end as the finale
Families waited all day for the youngest division's awardsAwards handed out as each division finishes
Warm-ups near the judges blocked the viewA marked practice zone and a clear line of sight to the stage
Restrooms and the right entrance were hard to findSignage for check-in, restrooms, food and vendors

VENUE RULES FOR VENDORS & SPONSORS
SETUP / TEARDOWN
Setup before 10 AM (before mall opens). Everything out by 7 PM. Hand trucks with rubber wheels only.
FLOOR ITEMS
Rubber mats or carpet pads under anything touching the mall floor. No tape on floor or fixtures.
SIGNAGE
All banners/signs need written DTC approval at least 2 weeks out. We handle the process — keep it professional.

GETTING THERE

Directions from Area Airports

Drive times are approximate and may vary with traffic. Free parking is available at all mall entrances.

FROM WASHINGTON DULLES INTERNATIONAL (IAD) — 13 min · 7.9 mi via VA-28 N
1. Head south on Saarinen Circle from the airport terminal.
2. Keep right onto Dulles Airport Access Road toward Washington (0.4 mi).
3. Take the exit onto VA-28 North / Sully Road (5.3 mi).
4. Turn right onto Dulles Town Circle. Mall entrance on your right.
FROM RONALD REAGAN NATIONAL (DCA) — 38 min · ~30 mi via I-66 W / VA-267 W
1. Take the George Washington Memorial Parkway north toward I-66 West.
2. Merge onto I-66 West toward Front Royal (8.5 mi).
3. Take VA-267 West / Dulles Toll Road toward Dulles Airport (17 mi).
4. Take Exit 9A onto VA-28 North / Sully Road (3.2 mi).
5. Turn right onto Dulles Town Circle. Mall entrance on your right.
FROM BALTIMORE/WASHINGTON INTERNATIONAL (BWI) — ~1 hr 10 min · 59 mi via I-95 S / I-495 W
1. Take I-195 East from the airport to I-95 South (3 mi).
2. Merge onto I-95 South toward Washington, D.C. (25 mi).
3. Take I-495 West (Capital Beltway) toward Virginia (12 mi).
4. Take VA-267 West / Dulles Toll Road toward Dulles Airport (14 mi).
5. Take Exit 9A onto VA-28 North / Sully Road (3 mi).
6. Turn right onto Dulles Town Circle. Mall entrance on your right.

BWI is a major Southwest Airlines hub — often the cheapest option for East Coast travelers.

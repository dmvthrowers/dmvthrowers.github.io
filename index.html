ABOUT US

More Than a
Yo-Yo Club

DMV Throwers is the Washington DC/Maryland/Virginia region's yo-yo and skill toy community. We gather every 3rd Sunday of the month at Arlington Central Library — beginners, families, and competitors all welcome.

Whether you've never touched a yo-yo or you're grinding competition routines, this is your crew. We have loaner yo-yos, experienced players to learn from, and a genuinely welcoming community.

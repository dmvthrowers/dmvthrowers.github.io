FAQ

Common Questions

Is the event free to attend?+
Yes — free to spectate. Competitor entry is $15–25 per division depending on division. No charge for spectators, families, or curious mall visitors.
How do I register to compete?+
Registration opens soon. Email vastateyoyocontest@gmail.com to get notified first. Registration is only complete after payment clears.
Can I compete in multiple divisions?+
Yes. Registration is per division. Register for 1A + X Division together for $40 ($5 off the combined price).
What is the Maker Corner?+
A community showcase area for handmade skill toys, custom designs, and indie crafted work. Not a sponsor table — this is community space for makers. Contact us if you're interested in a spot.
Will there be a livestream?+
Planned — pending funding. Diamond and Platinum sponsors receive YouTube livestream credit. Once funded, it's happening. Help us get there.
Where do I park?+
Free parking at all mall entrances. Dulles Town Center has ample surface parking surrounding the entire mall. No fees, no validation needed.
How do I become a sponsor?+
Fill out the sponsor interest form or email vastateyoyocontest@gmail.com. Diamond and Platinum spots are limited — first come, first served. You'll receive a PayPal invoice to lock your spot.
Who organizes VSYC-26?+
DMV Throwers Yo-Yo & Skill Toy Club — the DC/Maryland/Virginia yo-yo community club. Entirely volunteer-run. Every dollar goes straight to the event.

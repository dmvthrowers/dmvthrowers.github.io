How to read these scores: every score is out of 100. In 1A and X, that is up to 60 for Technical Execution (clickers) and 40 for Routine Evaluation, minus major deductions (stop −1, discard −3, detach −5). Sport / Beginner / Junior is 20 for Technical Execution and 80 for evaluation, with no negative clicks or deductions. Scores are averaged across the judges. Full scoring breakdown →

Stella Duellum

Virginia State Yo-Yo Contest brought to you by Goodles · September 19, 2026 · Sterling, VA

GUEST EVENT

Stella Duellum · Prismatic Stars

Stella Duellum returns to VA States. Hosted by Anneurismz for Prismatic Stars, it has its own sign-up and its own rules, running alongside our divisions. It starts at 12:00 PM.

HOW STELLA DUELLUM WORKS
  • One-minute routines, head to head
  • Music is random — you hear it when the crowd does
  • Wait off stage until your turn is called
  • You cannot repeat the same routine twice. Repeating a routine is an instant disqualification.
  • The winner of each round is decided by a live chat poll on the YoYo Contest Central YouTube stream
HOW TO ENTER
  • Sign-up is run by Prismatic Stars on Challonge, separately from VSYC-26 registration
  • Questions go to Prismatic.stars.art@gmail.com — not to our contest inbox

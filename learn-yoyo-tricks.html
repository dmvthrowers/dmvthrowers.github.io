The First 100 Tricks

One path through the basics, from your first throw to every style of play. Each name links to a free tutorial, most from the YoYoTricks.com library, which runs from first throw to master level.[15][17][13] Gravity Pull links to Yomega’s video list.[29] Skip around; there’s no wrong order.

Prefer a shorter list? Our own Trick Reference Guide slides start with 16 beginner tricks on a responsive yo-yo, then group intermediate and expert tricks into families by mount.[2]

Part 7

A Contest-Tested Ladder

A trick ladder is a set list of tricks in rising difficulty, used at contests to ease newer players into competition.[23] The 2000 World Yo-Yo Contest ran a 30-trick sport ladder in three levels: one try per trick, one miss forgiven.[23] It still makes a great checklist.

2000 World Yo-Yo Contest Sport Ladder (simplified)[23]
#TrickTo pass
1Forward PassPalm-up catch
2Walk the DogAt least one foot forward
3Rock the BabyThree full rocks
4Around the WorldOne time
5Around the CornerTug the string with your fingers
6LoopsThree inside loops
7UFOPick it up with the other hand
8Hop the FenceThree repetitions
9Tidal WaveFingers touch at the start
10Man on TrapezeNo flip-overs
11PinwheelOver the thumb, three rotations
12RestartFull stop, full rewind
13ElevatorHop off at the top
14Three-Leaf CloverLast loop inside
15Brain TwisterTwo somersaults, loop ending
16Double or NothingAny dismount
17SidewinderBoth sides
18Time WarpForward to reverse around the world
19Dizzy BabyThree rocks, then three pinwheels
20Split the AtomThree somersaults, loop ending
21Reach for the MoonFive repetitions
22Vertical LoopsFive repetitions
23Warp DriveThree regenerated worlds
24Mach 5At least five spins
25One-Hand FountainThree reps each
26Triple TrapezeTrapeze, then regen trapeze
27Triple or NothingRewind dismount
28Advanced Split the AtomBarrel rolls and somersaults
29Hydrogen BombFerris wheel dismount
30Roller Coaster (McBride)Coaster mount and double or nothing

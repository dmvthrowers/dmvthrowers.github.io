FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Got a question? Chances are it's answered below. If not, reach us at contact@dmvthrowers.club or @DMV_THROWERS on Instagram.

General
What is DMV Throwers?+
DMV Throwers is a free, community-run yo-yo and skill toy club serving the Washington DC, Maryland, and Virginia area. We bring together throwers of all ages and experience levels — from total beginners to national competitors. Our goal is simple: build a fun, welcoming community around yo-yo and skill toys.
Who runs the club?+
The club was founded by Arthur Foley (President/Founder) and is coordinated day-to-day by Brandon Rogers (Vice President). Our current officer team also includes Abby Brodsky (Co-Coordinator). We're a volunteer-run organization.
Is there a membership fee or dues?+
No. Attending our events is completely free. We accept voluntary donations if you'd like to support the club, but there are no required fees, dues, or memberships of any kind.
Does the club have social media?+
Yes! Find us at Instagram: @DMV_THROWERS, Facebook: DMV Throwers, Email: contact@dmvthrowers.club, and Linktree: linktr.ee/dmvthrowers
Is DMV Throwers a nonprofit?+
Not currently. We operate as a community club. We are exploring nonprofit status as a future step, which would allow us to expand programming and apply for grants. The charter would be updated if and when that changes.
Meetup Logistics
When and where do you meet?+
We hold monthly meetups at Arlington Central Library — Barbara M. Donnellan Auditorium / Bluemont Room, 1015 N Quincy St, Arlington, VA 22201. During warmer months we sometimes hold outdoor meetups at Quincy Park in Arlington, VA.
Do I need to register or RSVP?+
For special events we may request an RSVP to help with planning — if so, it will be noted in the event announcement. Registration links are provided for major events.
Is there parking?+
Yes. Street parking is available on N Quincy St and surrounding streets. The library is about a 5-minute walk from the Virginia Square–GMU Metro station on the Orange and Silver lines.
What time should I arrive?+
Anytime between 1 and 4 PM works. You don't need to arrive right at 1 — people come and go throughout. If it's your first time, arriving a little earlier gives you more time to get settled and meet people.
What if a meetup is cancelled?+
Cancellations will be announced as early as possible on Instagram (@DMV_THROWERS) and our Facebook page. Always check social media close to the event date if you're unsure.
Skill Levels & What to Bring
Do I need to know how to yo-yo to come?+
Not at all. Total beginners are just as welcome as experienced players. If you've never thrown a yo-yo before, we'll help you get started. There's no performance requirement — just come and have fun.
Do I need to bring my own yo-yo?+
No. We have loaner yo-yos available at every meetup. If you own one, bring it — but if you don't, that's completely fine.
What yo-yo should I buy if I want to get one?+
That's a great question to ask at a meetup! Our members have a wide range of experience and are happy to give recommendations based on your budget and style. We'd rather give you in-person advice than a generic answer.
Is it just yo-yos, or other skill toys too?+
All skill toys are welcome. Kendama, diabolo, juggling, and other flow/skill toys are all fair game at our meetups. The club's focus is yo-yo but we're a welcoming space for the broader skill toy community.
What should I wear or bring?+
Wear comfortable clothes you can move in. Beyond that, just bring yourself — and your yo-yo if you have one. We'll handle the rest.
Kids & Families
Is the club family-friendly?+
Absolutely. DMV Throwers is designed to be welcoming for families. We regularly have kids and parents attend together, and we make sure the environment is positive and inclusive for all ages.
Is there an age minimum?+
No official age minimum. We welcome all ages. Younger children should be accompanied by a parent or guardian. For formal officer roles, we do require members to be 18 or older.
Can my child attend without me?+
Older teens are welcome to attend independently. For younger children, we recommend a parent or guardian be present, particularly for their first visit.
Are loaner yo-yos safe for kids?+
Yes. Our loaner yo-yos are appropriate for a range of ages. If you have concerns about a specific yo-yo for a younger child, just ask an officer at the event and we'll point you to the right one.
Contests & VSYC-26
What is VSYC-26?+
VSYC-26 is the Virginia State YoYo Contest 2026 — a competitive yo-yo event we are actively working to host in the DMV region. It would be a sanctioned state-level contest bringing competitive players from across Virginia and the surrounding region together. Learn more →
Do I have to be competitive to come to the club?+
Not at all. The vast majority of our members come to socialize, learn tricks, and have fun — not to compete. Competition is one part of the broader community we're building, not a requirement.
Will there be a fee to enter VSYC-26?+
Competitive contests typically involve a participant registration fee to cover event costs. There may also be a small spectator fee depending on the venue. Any fees will be clearly communicated well in advance and kept as low as possible. Regular monthly meetups will always remain free.
How do I stay updated on VSYC-26?+
Follow @DMV_THROWERS on Instagram and join our Facebook group. All announcements, venue updates, and registration info will be posted there first.
Does the club attend other contests?+
Members individually attend regional and national yo-yo contests, and we support those efforts as a community. As the club grows we may organize group attendance at events like nationals or regionals.
Getting More Involved
How do I become a member?+
Just show up! There's no application or sign-up process. Anyone who attends a DMV Throwers event is part of the community. If you want to get more involved in running things, read on.
Can I volunteer to help at events?+
Yes — and we'd love it. If you're a regular attendee and want to help with setup, greeting newcomers, running activities, or other tasks, just let Brandon know at a meetup or reach out at contact@dmvthrowers.club.
Are there open officer roles?+
Yes. We currently have two open officer positions: Alternate Location Co-Coordinator — leads logistics for non-primary venues, and Moderator/Activity Coordinator — plans in-event activities and community programming. If you're interested in either role, reach out to Brandon at contact@dmvthrowers.club.
Can I suggest ideas for events or activities?+
Absolutely. We're always open to ideas. Bring them up at a meetup, DM us on Instagram, or email contact@dmvthrowers.club. We can't promise everything will happen immediately, but we genuinely consider all suggestions.
Can my organization partner with DMV Throwers?+
Yes. We're open to venue partnerships, co-promotions, sponsorships, and community collaborations. Reach out to Brandon at contact@dmvthrowers.club with your idea and we'll go from there.

Yo-Yo Trick Tally: Statistics

Practice three tricks, keep score like a scientist, and turn your results into math!

Name: ______________________________Date: ____________

How to do it

  1. Pick a trick (try the Sleeper, Forward Pass, or Walk the Dog).
  2. Attempt the trick 20 times. Each time you land it, make a tally mark (❚❚❚❚ ❚) in the box.
  3. Count your tally marks and fill in the rest of the row.
Example: You try the Sleeper 20 times and land it 12 times.
Success rate = 12 out of 20 = 12/20 = 3/5 (simplified) = 60%

Think about it

1. You land the Sleeper 15 out of 20 tries. What percent is that?
2. You land the Forward Pass 8 out of 20 tries. Write the success rate as a fraction in simplest form.
3. Your friend lands Walk the Dog 19 out of 20 tries. What percent is that?
✂ Teacher Answer Key (cut or fold under before copying)
1. 75% — 15 ÷ 20 = 0.75  •  2. 2/5 — 8/20 simplifies to 2/5  •  3. 95% — 19 ÷ 20 = 0.95

Yo-Yo Physics Lab: Energy on a String

A yo-yo is a working lesson in potential energy, kinetic energy, and friction. You will need: one yo-yo, a meter stick or tape measure, and a stopwatch (a phone works).

Given: yo-yo mass m = 65 g = 0.065 kg  ·  gravity g = 9.8 m/s²  ·  potential energy PE = m·g·h  ·  kinetic energy KE = ½m·v²

4. A yo-yo string is 50 inches long. The coach trims off 10% of it. How many inches are trimmed off, and how long is the string now?

5. Of 25 contest tricks, a player landed 20. What percent of the tricks did the player land?

✂ Teacher Answer Key (cut or fold under before copying)
1. 12:20 = 3/5 = 60% (12÷4=3, 20÷4=5; 3÷5=0.60)2. 3:2 (18÷6=3, 12÷6=2)
3. 3 tricks per minute (15÷5=3)4. 5 in. trimmed; 45 in. left (0.10×50=5; 50−5=45)
5. 80% (20/25=4/5=0.80)6. 2:5 (4÷2=2, 10÷2=5)
7. $8 per yo-yo (24÷3=8)8. $10 off; $30 sale price (0.25×40=10; 40−10=30)

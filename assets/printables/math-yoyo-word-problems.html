✂ Teacher Answer Key (cut or fold under before copying)
1. 15 tricks (6 + 9 = 15)2. 17 members (24 − 7 = 17)
3. 20 strings (5 × 4 = 20)4. $2 ($10 − $8 = $2)
5. $18 (3 × $6 = $18)6. 36 inches (42 − 6 = 36)
7. 4 yo-yos (24 ÷ 6 = 4)8. 7 yo-yos (15 − 5 − 3 = 7)

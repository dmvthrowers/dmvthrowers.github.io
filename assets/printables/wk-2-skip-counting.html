Yo-Yo Skip Counting

2nd Grade · Fill in the missing numbers as you skip count. Then solve the word problems.

Name: ______________________________Date: ____________

1. Skip count by 2s:

2, 4, 6,  , 10,  , 14,  

2. Skip count by 5s:

5, 10,  , 20,  , 30

3. Skip count by 10s:

10, 20, 30,  ,  , 60

4. Skip count by 2s, starting at 12:

12, 14,  , 18,  , 22

5. Each pack holds 5 yo-yo strings. The club buys 4 packs. Count by 5s to find how many strings:

5, 10, 15,    =  ______ strings

6. Zoe practices 2 new tricks each day for 5 days. Count by 2s to find how many tricks:

2, 4, 6, 8,    =  ______ tricks

✂ Teacher Answer Key (cut or fold under before copying)
1. 8, 12, 162. 15, 25
3. 40, 504. 16, 20
5. 20 strings (4 × 5)6. 10 tricks (5 × 2)

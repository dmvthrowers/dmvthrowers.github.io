Virginia State Yo-Yo Contest · September 19, 2026 · Sterling, VA

CONTEST RULES

Contest Ruleset

FREESTYLE FORMAT
  • Freestyles follow NYYL/IYYF guidelines — view full NYYL ruleset
  • 1A, X Division: 2-minute freestyle with music
  • Sport / Beginner / Junior (single combined division): 90-second format — no negative clickers, low-pressure division
  • No profanity or offensive content in music — disqualification applies
EQUIPMENT
  • Competitors must use division-legal yo-yos for their registered division
  • String and accessories are competitor's responsibility
  • Inspections may occur before competition at judge's discretion
MUSIC & PERFORMANCE
  • Music files due by September 12 — no exceptions after deadline
  • File format: MP3, named DIVISION_Last_First.mp3
  • No live music — pre-recorded tracks only
  • Competitors must be ready when called — missing your slot forfeits your spot
JUDGING & CONDUCT
  • Judging follows NYYL scoring criteria — view full ruleset
  • Judge decisions are final
  • Respectful conduct required — unsportsmanlike behavior may result in disqualification
  • All competitors and attendees are expected to follow the DMV Throwers Code of Conduct →
  • Venue rules apply to all competitors, spectators, and staff
  • Organizers reserve the right to adjust format based on registration numbers

★ Full detailed ruleset will be distributed to registered competitors closer to the event date. Rulesets and competition rules may be modified up to one month prior to the event. Questions? Email vastateyoyocontest@gmail.com

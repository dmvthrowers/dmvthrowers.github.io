Apps & Tools

  • Level Up! (iOS) · AndroidYoYoTricks.com's free app. Stream tutorials and track the tricks you've landed.
  • Skill Addicts (iOS) · AndroidGamified trick learning in your pocket.
  • YoYo Pro Clicker (iOS) · AndroidCount freestyle deductions like a contest judge. Forum thread.
  • ThrowLog (iPad)Log attempts, track streaks, and get told what to learn next. No account, data stays on your device.
  • NYYL Shadow JudgingScore live contest routines alongside the official panel, then compare your numbers. Free account required.
  • Yoyo CollectionCatalog your throws: specs, condition, value, and trade status. Free and open source, so you can host your own copy. Forum thread.
  • YOYOLOGFree collection tracker with photos, specs, and achievements. Install it to your phone’s home screen. No account, data stays on your device, and you can back it all up to a ZIP file. Brand new, so the maker wants feedback. Forum thread.
  • DimYoyoLine up yo-yos side by side to compare diameter, width, and gap before you buy. Open source on GitHub. Forum thread.
  • Yo-Yo DoodlerSketch a profile over a bearing and see it as a full yo-yo. A fun first step before CAD or a 3D print. Forum thread.

Books to Find

  • Look for these at your library or bookstore. Many yo-yo books are self-published by players, so libraries may not carry them; ask your librarian about a purchase request.
  • History & Collecting
  • Pedro’s Yo-YosRob Peñas, illustrated by Carl Angel. Lee & Low Books, 2024. A picture-book biography of Pedro Flores for young readers.
  • World on a StringHelane Zeiger, 1979. A history of the yo-yo.
  • Lucky’s Collectors Guide to 20th Century Yo-YosLucky J. Meisenheimer, 1999. History and values of classic yo-yos, by the director of the National Yo-Yo Hall of Fame.
  • Community & Self-Published
  • The People of Yoyo: Who We Are and Where to Find UsJ. D. “Mr. YoyoThrower” McKay, 2020. Interviews and stories from players about community, creativity, and yo-yo as a mental health tool.
  • Yoyo and Skill Toy Coloring BookJ. D. McKay, 2022. Coloring pages with short notes on yo-yos, kendama, spin tops, and other skill toys.
  • How to Run a Boutique Yoyo BusinessJ. D. McKay, 2019. Starting a small yo-yo company, from design and machine shops to marketing.
  • YoYo 2020: An Illustrated Guide to YoyoingKeisuke Saito, 2020. History, yo-yo structure, tricks, and yo-yoing as therapy.
  • The Yonomicon, second editionMark McBride, 2025. The science, theory, and history of yo-yo play; expands the 1998 original.
  • Yoyo Tricks for KidsJasper Todd, 2024. Thirty tricks with step-by-step illustrations, announced on the YoYoExpert forums.
  • Yin the Master of Yo!Pixel Pirate Studio, 2011. A self-published picture book by a yo-yo-playing animator.
  • Free to Read & Print
  • Yoyo Archive ZinesFree print-and-fold zines from the Yoyo Archive on the basics, strings, and more.
  • Yo-Yo Physics, Vols. 1–5Don Watson (“Captain Yo”). The science of yo-yos, free to read online.
  • Tricks & How-To
  • The Klutz Yo-Yo BookJohn Cassidy, 1987. A beginner trick book.
  • Awesome Yo-Yo TricksShar Levine and Bob Bowden, 2000. Yo-yo history plus simple to advanced tricks.
  • Yo-Yo TricksCynthia Fitterer Klingel, 2002. History and step-by-step tricks for young readers.
  • Amazing Yo-Yo TricksStuart Crump, 2000. By a National Yo-Yo Hall of Fame member.
  • Advanced Yo-Yo TricksBruce Weber, 1999. Diagrams for more than twenty advanced tricks.
  • Yo-Yo World Trick BookHarry Baier, 2000.
  • The Yonomicon, first editionMark McBride, 1998. A book on yo-yo theory that describes a system for writing down tricks.
  • Stories
  • The Giant Yo-Yo MysteryGertrude Chandler Warner, 2006. A Boxcar Children mystery about building the world’s largest yo-yo.
  • Yo-Yo ManDaniel Pinkwater, 2006. A third grader sets his sights on a yo-yo tournament.
  • The Yo-Yo ProphetKaren Krossing, Orca Book Publishers, 2011. A teen novel about a street-performing yo-yoer (grades 8–12).
  • Players’ bookshelvesA YoYoExpert thread where players share photos of the yo-yo books they own and which ones are worth tracking down.
  • More yo-yo booksThe YoYo Wiki keeps a longer list, including books that mention yo-yos.

History

  • The Story of the Yo-YoOur sourced timeline, from ancient Greece to today’s world contests, with the DC-area chapters marked.
  • Yo-Yo Science & DIYHow yo-yos work: physics, design, patents, and manufacturing, plus free 3D-printable designs and local makerspaces. Every fact cited.
  • Collecting Yo-YosOur sourced guide to collecting: Cheerio and Russell Coca-Cola yo-yos, limited runs and prototypes, condition and value, safe trading, storage, and yo-yo museums.
  • Filipino Yo-Yo HistoryRobert Reyes’s exhibit on Pedro Flores, the Duncan demonstrators, Joe Radovan, and Tommy Reyes.
  • National Yo-Yo MuseumThe world's largest public yo-yo display. Free to visit in Chico, CA.
  • Doc Lucky's History of the Yo-YoFrom ancient Greece to the modern contest scene.
  • Smithsonian: Yo-Yo HeritageThe National Museum of American History, right here in DC, holds the Duncan company archives.
  • Duncan Family Yo-Yo CollectionThe Duncan family's yo-yo collection, preserved in the archives of the National Museum of American History in DC. It includes 35 photographs of Tommy Reyes, donated by his son Robert in 2017.
  • Bob Rule PapersSmithsonian archive of a Hall of Fame yo-yo and top demonstrator, including a file on Filipino demonstrator Gus Somera.
  • The Legacy of Tommy ReyesFilipino American Duncan champion of 1932 who later did yo-yo spots on DC and Baltimore kids' TV, including Bozo with Willard Scott. National Yo-Yo Hall of Fame. Read the exhibit.
  • Dick Stohr, "That Yo-Yo Guy"Springfield, VA performer, six-time world record setter, and longtime contest judge. He played the National Theatre in DC in 2003. Profiled in The Washington Post. More on the YoYo Wiki.
  • Ann Connolly (Bunake)DC-raised and one of the best-known women in yo-yo since the early 2000s. World Contest finalist. Learn her tricks on YoYoTricks.com.
  • Tom ConnollyAnn's dad, spin top standout, and collector with a massive collection. Watch him at the 2012 World Spin Top Competition.
  • Virginia State Contest HistoryVA States first ran on January 21, 2006. The first results include Ann and Tom Connolly. VSYC-26 carries it forward.
  • Jonathan Robinson (JonRob)5A player from Lynchburg, VA, and 2007 Trick Innovator of the Year at U.S. Nationals.
  • Mikhail TulabutVirginia string-trick innovator, Team YoYoJam member, and co-founder of Southeast Virginia Skillz Club (2005).
  • World Record at Fort A.P. HillThe Guinness record for most people yo-yoing at once: 2,036 people at the 2010 National Scout Jamboree in Caroline County, VA.
  • Capital YoYo ClubAn earlier DC, MD, and NoVA club. In 2012 it met the first Saturday of each month, with meets at Union Station, while a Maryland group met at Baker Park in Frederick. Its forum thread is a snapshot of local yo-yo history. The YoYo Wiki notes many of its members now throw with DMV Throwers.
  • Yo-Yo Share, Show & PracticeA Northern Virginia meetup listed on the YoYo Wiki, held the third Wednesday of each month at Luther Jackson Middle School in Merrifield, VA, with Dick Stohr and Tom Connolly as contacts.
  • Mid-Atlantic Regional (MAR)First held in 2000, it’s one of the U.S. regionals that predates the National Yo-Yo League. For years it was hosted by Mitchell’s Trains, Toys & Hobbies in Wilmington, DE.
  • Pedro FloresThe Filipino American maker who brought the yo-yo to the U.S. in the 1920s.
  • Pedro and the Yo-Yo: Man and MythPositively Filipino on Flores and the Filipino demonstrators who carried the 1930s craze.
  • The Yoyo: A Story of Downs and UpsPositively Filipino profile of Fred Urian, one of the Filipino demonstrators the Flores brothers trained in Chicago in 1932.
  • Royal Tops and the Word “Yo-Yo”Filipino demonstrators Joe Radovan and Ruben Delagana founded Royal, which won the 1965 case that made “yo-yo” a common word.
  • Donald F. Duncan Sr.The businessman who bought Flores's company and sent demonstrator teams across the country. National Yo-Yo Day, June 6, is his birthday.
  • Duncan: A Rich History of a Simple ToyDuncan’s own history of the yo-yo, from ancient Greece to the Hearst newspaper contests and traveling demonstrators.
  • The Engines of Our Ingenuity: The Yo-YoA short University of Houston radio essay on Flores, Duncan, and who deserves credit for the yo-yo.
  • Yo-Yo Man (1978)Duncan's documentary featuring demonstrator Nemo Concepcion, one of the Filipino pros from the 1930s boom.
  • National Yo-Yo Hall of Fame VideosDoc Lucky's profiles of Hall of Fame members and nominees.
  • Yo-Yo on WikipediaA solid overview of the toy, its booms, and its players.
  • YoYo WikiFan-built wiki of players, companies, contests, and tricks.
